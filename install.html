Time Clock App

Add it to your phone once. Then it's just an icon you tap — no website, no login page.

Android YOUR PHONE

Tap the button. Your phone will ask if you want to install — say yes.

  1. Tap the ⋮ menu (three dots, top-right of Chrome).
  2. Tap Install app — or Add to Home screen.
  3. Tap Install to confirm.

Look for the Time Clock icon on your home screen when it's done.

iPhone / iPad YOUR PHONE

You have to use Safari for this. If you're reading this in Chrome, Instagram, or Discord's built-in browser, it won't work — open it in Safari first.

  1. Tap the Share button — the square with an arrow pointing up. It's at the bottom of the screen, or top-right on an iPad.
  2. Scroll down the list and tap Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add in the top-right corner.

Look for the Time Clock icon on your home screen when it's done.

Computer

You don't really need to install it on a computer — the website works fine. But if you want to:

  1. In Chrome or Edge, look at the right end of the address bar.
  2. Click the little install icon (a screen with a down arrow).
  3. Click Install.

Questions

Is this a real app from the app store?
No — and that's on purpose. It installs straight from this page, so there's nothing to search for and nothing to pay for. It behaves like any other app once it's on your home screen.

Will it eat my storage or battery?
No. It's tiny and only does anything while you have it open.

Does it track my location?
No. It records the time you clock in and out, and the location you pick from the list — nothing from your phone's GPS.

Do I need my code?
Yes. Your manager gives you a 6-digit code, and you type it in to clock in or out. Ask your manager if you don't have one yet.

Do I need internet?
Yes. Your clock-ins have to reach the system to count, so you need cell service or wifi when you clock in and out.

It's not working.
Skip it and use the website — the button above works exactly the same. Then tell your manager.
